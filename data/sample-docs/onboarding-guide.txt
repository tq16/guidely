Employee Onboarding Guide

New employees must complete their onboarding tasks during their first week.

On the first day, collect your company laptop and access card from the office administrator. The IT team will help you sign in to your company account and configure multi-factor authentication.

During the first week, employees must complete the Security Awareness Training and read the Information Security Policy. New employees should also meet their manager to agree on their first-month goals.

Human Resources will provide information about payroll, working hours, leave requests, and company benefits.

If you have questions during onboarding, contact your manager or the Human Resources team.
