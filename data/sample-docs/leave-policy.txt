Annual Leave Policy

All full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees may request annual leave after completing three months of employment. Leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least seven calendar days before the requested start date.

Managers should respond to leave requests within three working days. Approval depends on team workload and business requirements.

Employees may carry forward up to five unused annual-leave days to the next calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used before 31 March, otherwise it expires.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and should not be requested as annual leave.
