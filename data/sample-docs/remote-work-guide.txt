Remote Work Guide

Employees may work remotely up to two days each week after approval from their direct manager.

Remote work should be planned with the team and recorded on the shared team calendar. Employees must remain available during their normal working hours and attend scheduled meetings.

Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems or company documents. Public Wi-Fi should not be used without the VPN.

If an employee needs a long-term remote work arrangement, they must submit a written request to Human Resources and their department manager.
