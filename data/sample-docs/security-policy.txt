Information Security Policy

All employees must use multi-factor authentication for company accounts and systems.

Passwords must contain at least 12 characters and should not be reused across company and personal accounts. Do not share passwords with any person, including colleagues or managers.

Suspicious emails, unexpected links, and unknown attachments should be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in the email system.

Company documents containing confidential information must only be stored in approved company systems. Employees must not send confidential documents to personal email addresses or upload them to personal cloud storage.

Any suspected data breach or lost company device must be reported to IT Security immediately.
