IT Support FAQ

How do I reset my company password?

Visit the company password portal and select "Reset Password". You will need to verify your identity using multi-factor authentication. If you cannot access the portal, contact the IT Service Desk.

How do I request a new laptop or accessory?

Submit an equipment request through the IT Service Desk portal. Standard requests are normally processed within five working days.

What should I do if my laptop is lost or stolen?

Report the issue immediately to IT Security and your manager. Do not wait until the next working day. IT will remotely protect company data where possible.

IT Service Desk hours are Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
